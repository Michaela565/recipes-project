Simple sesame pasta

Vegan

Simple sesame pasta done under 10 minutes

Preparation time: 10-15 minutes

Portion: 1

Ingredients

  • Noodles or Pasta
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Chicken powder
  • Sugar
  • Cumin
  • Chilly flakes
  • Vinegar
  • Olive oil
  • Sesame oil
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame seeds

Preparation

  1. prepare your noodles or pasta by boiling them
  2. cut your onion and garlic into small pieces
  3. mix your seasoning by mixing chilly powder, sugar, chicken powder, cumin and sesame seeds
  4. add onion and garlic to your mix
  5. pour sesame oil over it
  6. boil olive oil in a small pot or pan
  7. pour the boiling oil over your mix
  8. mix in soy sauce and vinegar
  9. mix your noodles/pasta and the mix together
  10. done and dusted
