Teriyaki tofu

Vegan

Teriyaki tofu without sake.

Preparation time: 20-30 minutes

Portion: 1 portion

Ingredients

  • Tofu
  • Noodles
  • Honey
  • Salt
  • Chilli
  • Oyster sauce
  • Water
  • Light soy sauce
  • Dark soy sauce
  • Sesame seeds
  • any oil

Preparation

  1. fry your tofu on a bit fo oil
  2. mix 2tbs of honey, 1tbs of dark soy sauce, 2tbs of light soy sauce, 1tbs spoon of oyster sause and 9 spoons of water
  3. while tofu is cooking, boil a pot of water and cook your noodles
  4. after your tofu is a bit fried pour in our teriyaki mix and let simmer on medium heat
  5. when most of the sauce dissappears, turn off the heat
  6. serve by mixing with noodles and sprinkling sesame seeds over
  7. done and dusted
