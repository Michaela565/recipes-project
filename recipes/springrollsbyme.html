Better spring rolls by me

Vegan

Spring rolls that are not fried on oil, filled with tofu, vegetables and noodles

Preparation time: 20-30 minutes

Portion: according to the amount of ingredients you choose

Ingredients

  • Rice paper
  • Bowl of water to wet our paper
  • Tofu
  • Seasoning up to your taste (I choose curry and chicken powder)
  • Fresh or frozen vegetables
  • Soy sauce
  • Sesame seeds

Preparation

  1. add seasoning to your tofu
  2. fry the tofu on a pan with a bit of oil on medium heat
  3. while tofu is cooking, boil a pot of water and cook your noodles
  4. after 3-4 minutes put the vegetables in
  5. put the rice paper in the bowl of water for 10 seconds
  6. take it out and put it on a plate
  7. start putting the tofu and noodles on the rice paper
  8. top it with soy sauce and sesame seeds
  9. close the rice paper
  10. put it on a preheated pan for 1-2 minutes for each side
  11. repeat these steps till you run out of ingredients
  12. done and dusted
